Cooling Patrol is the Voltic solution for wireless temperature monitoring in cold rooms, commercial refrigerators, and other refrigeration spaces. Sensors send readings to the [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es) platform, where you can review room status, configure alerts, check history, and prepare the HACCP record.

<Note>
  The screen names in this guide match the current interface. They may vary by user role, app version, or building configuration. The [installation guide](/en/cooling-patrol/installation) keeps the setup and connection procedure.
</Note>

## System components

| Component | Function |
| --- | --- |
| **Cooling Patrol Hub** | Receives sensor data and sends readings over Ethernet or Wi‑Fi. |
| **Cooling Patrol temperature sensors** | Measure the temperature inside each cold room and, when provided by the device, show humidity and battery status. |
| **app.voltic.es** | Shows the overview, room status, alerts, history, and HACCP reports. |

![Cooling Patrol sensor](https://cdn.voltic.es/media/cooling-patrol/product/front_nb.png)

## Workflow

1. The sensor takes a reading and sends it to the Cooling Patrol Hub.
2. The Hub sends the reading to [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es).
3. The app compares the reading with the configured range and shows states such as **In range**, **Close to limit**, **Out of range**, **No data**, **Alert off**, or **Not configured**.
4. Deviations are reviewed in **Cooling Patrol > Overview** and documented in **HACCP > Register** when required by the site's procedure.

<Warning>
  An old reading or **No data** status does not confirm that the room is at a safe temperature. If connectivity is lost, take a manual measurement and follow the site's food-safety plan.
</Warning>
