Cooling Patrol is the Voltic solution for wireless temperature monitoring in cold rooms, commercial refrigerators, and other refrigeration spaces. Sensors report temperature in real time through the [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es) platform, where you can configure alerts and review history.

## System components

- **Cooling Patrol Hub** (connects via Ethernet or Wi-Fi)
- **Cooling Patrol temperature sensors** (wireless, installed inside each cold room)
