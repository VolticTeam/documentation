## Capture criteria

- Use a demo building and demo sensors; remove customer names, addresses, serial numbers, email addresses, and personal data.
- Capture the normal state and, where possible, controlled examples of **Out of range**, **No data**, and **Offline** without changing a real installation.
- Keep the visible time zone, °C units, and exact control labels.
- Provide WebP or PNG images with the same crop and resolution for Spanish and English.
- Store the app version, user role, date, and publication permission with each image.

## Technical confirmations before publication

| Detail | Why it needs confirmation |
| --- | --- |
| Default values and maximum/minimum ranges for threshold, hysteresis, cooldown, and breach duration | The app shows the fields, but valid ranges and initial values may depend on version and stored product. |
| Notification channels, recipients, repetition, and escalation | The observed interface configures the alert, but it does not document here how notifications are sent or who receives them. |
| Reading cadence, timeout before **No data**, and reconnection behavior | This determines when a reading is no longer usable and what must be measured manually. |
| Hub startup time and the 30–60 minute wait after onboarding | These operational times appear in the installation guide and must be validated for the deployed hardware and firmware. |
| Sensor/battery type, capacity, life, polarity, replacement, and protection rating | These are not defined in this documentation and must not be inferred from other Voltic sensors. |
| Placement point, tolerance, interval, and calibration method; offset support | These metrology and installation requirements must come from the model manual and site procedure. |
| Definition of **Critical** versus **Warning** | The app shows both labels, but the operational criterion must match the HACCP plan. |
| Retention period, signature/lock, accepted format, backups, and reviewing authority | The app offers states and exports, but official-record validity depends on jurisdiction and the applicable procedure. |
| Permission matrix for managing alerts, resolving, and reviewing incidents | Visible controls change by role; validate who can perform each action. |

### Values observed in the interface (do not publish yet)

The current app validation shows the following limits and initial values. They are implementation references, not an approved specification:

| Field | Observed | Confirmation pending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alert enabled | A new form appears enabled. | Onboarding policy for already-installed sensors. |
| Minimum/maximum threshold | Inputs between −100 and 100 °C; maximum must be greater than minimum. | Product operating range and whether it matches the visual control. |
| Visual allowed range | The range slider shows −20 to 20 °C. | Resolve the difference with the −100 to 100 °C validation before documenting it. |
| Hysteresis | 0–50 °C; observed initial value: 0 °C. | Operational meaning and recommended value per installation. |
| Wait between alerts | 1–10,080 min; observed initial value: 60 min. | Minimum, maximum, and repetition policy. |
| Duration before incident | 0–1,440 min; observed initial value: 5 min. | Whether 0 is allowed for every model and when the record is created. |

<Warning>
  Until these points are closed, describe controls as **available in the observed interface**, not as requirements, defaults, or a compliance guarantee.
</Warning>
