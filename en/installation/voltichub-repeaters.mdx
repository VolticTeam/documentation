The VolticHub is the coordinator of the Zigbee network. Its correct installation is essential to ensure connectivity for all devices within the rooms. Always install it before mounting the devices in each room.

<Note>
  The recommended topology is one Hub per floor: approximately 10–15 rooms and up to about 150 devices per Hub as a reference. Rooms must remain on the same floor; use repeaters or an additional Hub when the coverage survey requires it.
</Note>

<Warning>
  This step is the first and most critical of the entire installation. Without the Hub properly connected, no sensor or actuator on the floor will work.
</Warning>

## Recommended location

- **Technical space or hallway ceiling** on the corresponding floor
- With **accessibility and clear visibility** (avoid metal cabinets or shielded areas)
- The antenna must be at **90 degrees (perpendicular to the hub)**

## VolticHub connection

<Warning>
  The power supply is 230V. The connection must be performed by a qualified electrician, with the circuit isolated, locked out, and verified, and in accordance with local regulations.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the RJ45 cable first">
    Connect the Ethernet cable (RJ45) to the Hub port before plugging in the power. This ensures the Hub boots with available network connectivity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the 230V power supply">
    Once the Ethernet is connected, connect the 230V power supply to the Hub. Wait a few minutes for the system to start correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Always connect the RJ45 before the power supply. If you reverse the order, the Hub may boot in Wi-Fi mode even if Ethernet is available.
</Note>

## Local operation without Internet

The VolticHub manages Zigbee communication and room automations locally. Therefore, the system can continue operating even when the Hub has no Internet access. A **communication error** shown for a room in the platform does not necessarily mean that the system has stopped working.

Internet access allows rooms to be viewed and controlled from [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es), as well as their information to be received by the platform.

## Diagnosing an offline Hub

<Steps>
  <Step title="Physically check the power and RJ45 cables">
    Go to the Hub installation location and verify that both the power cable and the network cable are properly connected. If the Ethernet RJ45 lights are blinking, there is a network link and activity: continue with step 2 to validate that the Hub and local automations are working. If the lights are not on, continue with step 3.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If the RJ45 lights blink, validate local operation">
    Access a room associated with the Hub and check that the expected behavior occurs: when presence is detected, the contactor enables power to the lighting and TV lines, and opening the window disables HVAC according to the configured delay. If everything responds correctly, the Hub and its local automations are working; if the platform still shows no information, contact the hotel's IT service to review the platform connection. If an automation does not respond, there may be a Hub system issue: contact Voltic Support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If the RJ45 lights are off, check the power supply">
    Disconnect the Hub power and connect its USB-C cable to a mobile phone as an initial check of the power supply and cable. If the phone charges, continue with step 4 to check the network; this test alone does not rule out a fault in the Hub connector or electronics. If it does not charge, check the outlet, power supply, and power cable. After correcting the issue, repeat step 1; if power cannot be restored, contact Voltic Support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If the phone charges, check the network with the hotel's IT service">
    Ask IT to check whether the Hub appears in their system. If it does not, they must check the network point where it is connected and confirm that the device is not blocked. After any correction, repeat step 1 and then step 2. If the Hub appears correctly but all rooms remain disconnected or show a communication error, contact Voltic Support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Zigbee repeaters

Zigbee repeaters extend network coverage to rooms far from the Hub. Apply the same location rules: clear area, accessible, and without metal cabinets. They require 24/7 power.
