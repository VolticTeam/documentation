The VolticHub is the coordinator of the Zigbee network. Its correct installation is essential to ensure connectivity for all devices within the rooms. Always install it before mounting the devices in each room.

<Note>
  The recommended topology is one Hub per floor: approximately 10–15 rooms and up to about 150 devices per Hub as a reference. Rooms must remain on the same floor; use repeaters or an additional Hub when the coverage survey requires it.
</Note>

<Warning>
  This step is the first and most critical of the entire installation. Without the Hub properly connected, no sensor or actuator on the floor will work.
</Warning>

## Recommended location

- **Technical space or hallway ceiling** on the corresponding floor
- With **accessibility and clear visibility** (avoid metal cabinets or shielded areas)
- The antenna must be at **90 degrees (perpendicular to the hub)**

## VolticHub connection

<Warning>
  The power supply is 230V. The connection must be performed by a qualified electrician, with the circuit isolated, locked out, and verified, and in accordance with local regulations.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the RJ45 cable first">
    Connect the Ethernet cable (RJ45) to the Hub port before plugging in the power. This ensures the Hub boots with available network connectivity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the 230V power supply">
    Once the Ethernet is connected, connect the 230V power supply to the Hub. Wait a few minutes for the system to start correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Always connect the RJ45 before the power supply. If you reverse the order, the Hub may boot in Wi-Fi mode even if Ethernet is available.
</Note>

## Zigbee repeaters

Zigbee repeaters extend network coverage to rooms far from the Hub. Apply the same location rules: clear area, accessible, and without metal cabinets. They require 24/7 power.
