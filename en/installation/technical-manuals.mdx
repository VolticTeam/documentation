## Recommended tools for installation

Prepare these tools before starting:

- Screwdriver
- Level or mounting templates
- Caulking gun (with Sikaflex-118 Extreme Grab for contact sensors)
- Multimeter and battery tester
- Minimum recommended wiring (consult each device's diagrams)
