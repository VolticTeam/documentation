The Voltic Contact Sensor PRO is the same model for door and window. It detects opening and closing via a magnetic sensor and allows the system to turn off HVAC when an open window is detected.

## Sensor identification

The inner packaging label indicates the installation type:

- **P** = Door (Puerta)
- **V** = Window (Ventana)

## Window sensor

### Placement

- Place the sensor on the window's fixed frame.
- Place the magnet on the moving sash.
- The sensor's triangle must face the magnet.
- Distance between sensor and magnet: between 2 and 5 mm. On magnetic surfaces, keep a distance of 1 to 3 mm. Never exceed 5 mm.

## Door sensor

### Placement

- Place the sensor on the door's fixed frame.
- Place the magnet on the moving leaf.
- The sensor's triangle must face the magnet.
- Distance between sensor and magnet: between 2 and 5 mm. On magnetic surfaces, keep a distance of 1 to 3 mm. Never exceed 5 mm.

## Mounting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply adhesive">
    Apply Sikaflex-118 Extreme Grab over the 3M adhesive base of the sensor and the magnet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press">
    Press the sensor and magnet firmly for 10 seconds against the installation surface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alternative with screws">
    If the surface does not accept adhesive or requires greater resistance, use screws to fix the bases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Power supply

The sensor runs on 2 included AAA batteries. Open the compartment and verify polarity before closing it.

## Common mistakes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Reversing sensor and magnet">
    If you place the magnet on the fixed frame and the sensor on the moving leaf, the device may not work correctly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not respecting the 5 mm limit">
    A distance greater than 5 mm between sensor and magnet prevents detection of the closed state.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sensor on the moving leaf">
    The sensor must always go on the fixed part. Placing it on the moving leaf exposes the wiring to breakage from movement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not aligning the triangle">
    The sensor's triangle must face the magnet. If they are not aligned, the magnetic contact will be erratic.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not applying Sikaflex">
    Without Sikaflex-118 Extreme Grab, the 3M adhesive may not withstand continuous use on irregular surfaces.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Installing too close to the locking mechanism (door)">
    Keep the sensor away from the latch and hinges to avoid mechanical and magnetic interference.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Verification

The sensor's green LED blinks when opening or closing the door or window. If it does not react:

- Check that the batteries are properly placed and charged.
- Check that the distance between sensor and magnet is correct.
- Verify Zigbee network coverage in the area.
- Sync the sensor on [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es) if it does not appear on the platform.
