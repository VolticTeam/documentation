The Voltic FrostLine is a smart digital Zigbee thermostat for controlling 2-pipe / 2-way fan coils. It enables local and remote control, temperature measurement, time scheduling, and presence-based automation.

![Voltic Frostline 2 - Install thermostat](https://r2.voltic.es/media/installation-docs-assets/devices/frostline/conectando-termostato.webp)

## Where to install it

Install the Voltic FrostLine **inside the room**, following these rules:

- At a **height of 1.5 m** from the floor
- **Away from** windows, doors, radiators, splits, and exterior walls
- Always connected to a **24h electrical line** (not controlled by a contactor or key card)
- On a **standard flush-mounting box** of 75x75x35 mm (compatible with 86x86 frame)
- Do not install in corners, behind furniture, or in places where air circulation is obstructed

<Warning>
  Disconnect the power supply before mounting the thermostat. Do not touch the internal electronic board during installation.
</Warning>

## Connection and wiring

- **Power supply:** 230V AC / 50 Hz (exclusively via 230V line, no batteries required)
- **Maximum load:** 3A
- **Digital outputs:**
  - Valve: 2-way (also compatible with 3-way valve)
  - Fan speeds: V1 / V2 / V3

![Voltic Frostline 2 - Connection diagram (2-pipe)](https://r2.voltic.es/media/installation-docs-assets/devices/frostline/frostline_scheme.webp)

<Warning>
  Connect only 230V valves. Do not connect 12V or 24V valves to the Voltic FrostLine.
</Warning>

## Mounting

- Direct mounting on universal flush-mounting box
- Screws included in the kit
- Ensure leveling for correct front panel fitting

## Common mistakes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Tilted mounting">
    A tilted mounting creates poor contact with the box and can cause incorrect temperature readings. Make sure the thermostat is perfectly level.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Incorrect output wiring">
    If the digital output wiring is incorrect, the valve or fan will not respond. Verify the connection diagram before energizing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="12V or 24V valves connected">
    The Voltic FrostLine is only compatible with 230V valves. Connecting low-voltage valves may damage the device.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Post-installation checks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the power supply">
    Confirm that the thermostat receives 230V at the input terminals and that the screen responds correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the valve and fan">
    Verify that the thermostat controls the valve and fan speeds according to the active mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync with the platform">
    Confirm on [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es) that the device appears synced and reports temperature correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
