Proper temperature configuration is essential to balance guest comfort with energy efficiency. This page lists the recommended ranges for each season and their impact on consumption.

## Recommended temperatures

| Season | Recommended temperature |
| --- | --- |
| Summer (cooling) | 24°C - 26°C |
| Winter (heating) | 21°C - 23°C |

### Cooling (summer)

The range of **24°C to 26°C** provides thermal comfort without generating unnecessary consumption.

<Warning>
  Every 1°C below 24°C can increase energy consumption by approximately **6% to 8%**.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The 6–8% range is a reference based on internal Voltic data, not a guarantee for every installation. See [voltic.es](https://voltic.es) for more information.
</Note>

### Heating (winter)

The range of **21°C to 23°C** is optimal. Temperatures above 23-24°C can lead to higher energy consumption, a sensation of an overly dry environment, and lower system efficiency.

## Impact on energy consumption

Correct system operation can generate significant improvements in hotel energy efficiency.

| Action | Impact |
| --- | --- |
| Use of ECO mode | Reduces unnecessary HVAC operation |
| Appropriate temperatures | Lowers energy consumption |
| Window control | Prevents thermal losses |
| Automatic presence control | Optimizes system operation |

Hotels that operate the Voltic system correctly can achieve **energy savings of up to 30% in rooms**. This is an internal Voltic figure; see [voltic.es](https://voltic.es) for more information. Results depend on each installation's conditions.

## Key recommendations

- Prioritize **ECO mode** as the standard configuration when the room is unoccupied
- Use **COMFORT mode** in specific rooms when necessary
- Keep setpoint temperatures within the recommended ranges
- Periodically review alerts and system status at [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es)
- Avoid manipulating or blocking sensors
