## What to include in your inquiry

So we can help you as soon as possible, include the following information in your message:

- Hotel name
- Room number or affected device
- Problem description
- Screenshots from the app [app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es) if you have them
- Device serial number (if applicable, it is on the kit label)

<Tip>
  For urgent temperature issues in refrigeration chambers (Cooling Patrol), call the support phone directly instead of sending an email.
</Tip>

## Voltic Platform

Before contacting support, you can check the status of your devices at:

[app.voltic.es](https://app.voltic.es)

From the platform you can view active alerts, the synchronization status of each device, and the temperature or consumption history.
