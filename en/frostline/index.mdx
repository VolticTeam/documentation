Voltic FrostLine is a 230V Zigbee 3.0 thermostat with local and remote control, three fan speeds, and a European-standard socket. It can be installed as a standalone product in a home or integrated into Hotel Room Control.

It is also sold to consumers, including through Amazon. This section covers standalone installations; for a complete hotel deployment, see [Hotel Room Control](/en/getting-started).

See the [public FrostLine product page](https://voltic.es/en/product/frostline) for purchase information and product resources.

<Note>
  The connection diagrams in this documentation do not replace the manual supplied with the device. 230V installation must be performed by a qualified electrician.
</Note>

## Compatible Hubs

| Hub | Platform | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VolticHub** | app.voltic.es | Manage the thermostat within the Voltic ecosystem. |
| **Tuya-compatible Zigbee Hub** | Tuya Smart / Smart Life | Pair and control the thermostat through Tuya; available features depend on the Hub and its firmware. |

Choose the Hub before installation so you can prepare the correct network, pairing process, and user account.
