Voltic FrostLine can be used in several HVAC scenarios. The exact connection depends on the device version, firmware, installed actuators, and the control logic defined for the installation.

## Compatibility matrix

| Scenario | Compatibility | Validate before connecting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2-pipe** fan coil | Yes | This documentation's standard scheme: one valve and three fan speeds. |
| **4-pipe** fan coil | Yes, with a specific configuration | Independent heating and cooling valves, output assignment, and changeover logic according to the approved scheme for your version. |
| Fan coil + **hydronic underfloor heating** | Yes, with a specific configuration | Underfloor actuator, circuit priorities, sensors, and thermal protections. Do not connect an electric floor-heating load directly without a validated diagram. |
| VolticHub | Yes | Pairing through the Voltic ecosystem and the corresponding account. |
| Tuya-compatible Zigbee Hub | Yes | Hub model compatibility, inclusion process, and features exposed by Tuya Smart / Smart Life. |

<Warning>
  The 2-pipe diagram is not a template for a 4-pipe installation or a combined underfloor-heating installation. Do not energize the device until you verify terminal mapping, loads, and protections against the manual for your version.
</Warning>

## Information required for an advanced project

Before approving an advanced installation, collect:

- Exact FrostLine reference and firmware
- Fan coil and valve diagram
- Underfloor-heating actuator type, if applicable
- Voltage and current for each load
- Selected Hub: VolticHub or Tuya
- Desired heating, cooling, fan, and priority sequence

If any of this information is missing, request the connection diagram from support before wiring the device.
