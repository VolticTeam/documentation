This section helps you understand the Voltic system before you begin installation: how automation works, what devices make up each kit, and what the process is from mounting to commissioning.

<Note>
  In this documentation, **Hotel Room Control** is also called **Voltic Freeline**. **Voltic FrostLine** is the thermostat used in the system. To install FrostLine as a standalone product, see [its dedicated guide](/en/frostline).
  For hotel thermostat deployment, see the [hotel installation guide](/en/installation/voltic-frostline-hotel) and [FrostLine operation guide](/en/operation/voltic-frostline-hotel).
</Note>
